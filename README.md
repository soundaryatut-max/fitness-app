### 🟧 **About the App**

**Pulse** is a **modern fitness application** designed in **Figma** to help users stay **healthy, active, and motivated** every day. The clean interface, bold colors, and inspiring visuals create an enjoyable fitness experience.

---

## 🎨 **Color Palette**

🟧 **Primary – #FF5A1F**
🔥 Represents **Energy, Action & Motivation**

🟦 **Secondary – #1F3B73**
💙 Creates a feeling of **Trust & Stability**

🟩 **Accent – #B4FF3C**
✨ Highlights **Goals, Progress & Success**

⬛ **Neutral Dark – #1A1A1A**
🖤 Improves **Readability & Contrast**

---

## 🏋️ **Design Inspiration**

👥 **Group Workouts** – Build motivation through teamwork.

🏋️ **Strength Training** – Improve fitness and muscle strength.

❤️ **Heart Rate Tracking** – Monitor health using wearable devices.

🧘 **Stretching & Yoga** – Increase flexibility and relaxation.

🥗 **Healthy Nutrition** – Encourage balanced eating habits.

📱 **Modern UI Design** – Clean, simple, and easy to use.

---

## 🎯 **App Goal**

✅ Help users **track workouts**
✅ Monitor **fitness progress**
✅ Build **healthy daily habits**
✅ Keep users **motivated to achieve their goals**

# 🌟 **"Small Steps Lead to Big Success."** 💪✨

**Typography suggestion for Figma:**

* **Heading:** Poppins Bold (Orange 🟧)
* **Subheadings:** Montserrat SemiBold (Blue 🟦)
* **Body Text:** Open Sans or Inter (Dark Gray ⬛)
* **Highlights/Keywords:** Accent Green 🟩 (#B4FF3C)

This combination will give your fitness app presentation a bright, modern, and professional look.
